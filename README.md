# Amazon India Sales \& Profit Analytics Dashboard

A portfolio-ready Excel dashboard for analyzing Amazon India sales performance across revenue, profit, products, order status, payments, fulfillment, and geography.

## Project Overview

This project converts a 10,000-record Amazon India sales dataset into a management dashboard for quick business decision-making.

## Dashboard Coverage

* Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Units Sold
* Average Order Value and Profit Margin
* Return Rate and Cancellation Rate
* Monthly Sales \& Profit Trend
* Category-wise Sales, Profit, and Units Sold
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Order Status distribution
* Payment Method performance
* Fulfillment performance
* State-wise Sales and geographic performance
* Category-wise Returns \& Cancellations

## Key Business Insights

* Electronics \& Mobiles is the dominant category by sales and profit.
* Laptop Backpack, Power Bank 20000mAh, 5G Smartphone, Wireless Earbuds, and Smartwatch are the leading products by sales.
* Maharashtra is the strongest state by sales, profit, and order volume.
* UPI is the leading payment method by sales and profit.
* Amazon (FBA) is the leading fulfillment method by sales and profit.
* Returns and cancellations are surfaced to help assess revenue leakage and operational performance.
